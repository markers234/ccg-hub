      Privacy Policy

      Last updated: . This is a basecamp-run site, not a registered business, and this policy is written for transparency — it is not formal legal advice, and it hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer.

      What we collect

      • Your Google account name/email, used to sign in and shown next to the username you choose.
      • The username you choose, and which basecamps/rooms you're a member of.
      • Messages posted in rooms, wheel options, and poll votes.
      • A random visitor identifier used to prevent double poll votes.

      What we don't collect

      • No passwords — sign-in is handled entirely by Google.
      • No payment information.
      • No tracking cookies or third-party analytics/advertising scripts.

      How it's stored and who can see it

      Basecamp and room content is visible only to that basecamp's members and its admin. Wheel options and polls are visible to anyone who opens this page.

      Admin controls

      A basecamp's creator (admin) chooses who is added, can create and delete rooms, and can see the member list. Room creators can delete rooms they created.

      Retention & deletion

      Content persists until a room or option is manually deleted. Deleting a room removes its messages.

      Children's privacy

      This site is intended for the people who were personally invited and is not directed at children under 13.

      Changes

      This policy may be updated as the site changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the current version.

      Contact

      Questions about your data on this site should go directly to whoever invited you (the site's owner/admin).
