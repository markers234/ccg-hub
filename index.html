    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: . This is a community-run friend-group site, not a registered business, and this policy is written for transparency — it is not formal legal advice, and it hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer.

    What we collect

    • Display name / username you choose in Account.
    • A random device identifier stored in your browser (localStorage), used only to remember your name and poll votes on that device.
    • Messages you post in Chat, options you add to the Wheel, and polls/votes you create.
    • Your color-theme choice (CCG or Smudge Society).

    What we don't collect

    • No passwords — this site has no password-based login, so none are requested or stored.
    • No email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information.
    • No tracking cookies or third-party analytics/advertising scripts.

    How it's stored and who can see it

    Chat messages, wheel options, and polls are stored in shared cloud storage tied to this page and are visible to anyone you share this page's link with — treat it like a group chat, not a private diary. Your display name and device identifier stay on your own device except when your name is shown next to something you post.

    Retention & deletion

    Content persists until manually removed (e.g. deleting a wheel option). You can clear your local profile anytime from the Account tab; this removes your saved name from your device but does not retroactively delete past messages.

    Children's privacy

    This site is intended for the friend group that was given the link and is not directed at children under 13.

    Changes

    This policy may be updated as the site changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the current version.

    Contact

    Questions about your data on this site should go directly to whoever shared the link with you (the site's owner/admin).
